# The Dock's start-up services, part 2 (macOS 26.7.2)

Eight Mach / XPC services the real Dock (`/System/Library/CoreServices/Dock.app`, macOS 26.7.2 25G332) looks up at start that pearOS did not have: who serves them on the Mac, the wire, what the Dock does with the answer or with the failure, and what pearOS now runs (github.com/pearOS-bsd/d, `darwin/<name>`).  Part 1 (windowmanager.external, wallpaper): `DOCK-WINDOWMANAGER-WALLPAPER.md`; tccd, opendirectoryd, distnoted: `TCC-WIRE.md`, `OPENDIRECTORY-DISTNOTED-WIRE.md`.

Evidence tags: **[captured]** seen on the wire on the Mac VM (Apple's client and Apple's server, `research/tools/dock-services-2/dcap.dylib`), **[decompiled]** read in the binary (Ghidra 12.1.4 headless / REA, Dock, usernoted, CarbonCore, DeviceManagement, the framework imports of the Dock), **[inferred]** not seen, chosen to be consistent with what was seen.

Tools (`research/tools/dock-services-2`, our code): `dsprobe.m` (makes the Dock's calls through Apple's frameworks, one word per service), `dcap.c` (interposer: every XPC connection, `xpc_connection_create` included, every message and reply, NSXPC payloads saved; `IS_REDIRECT=from=to` points Apple's client at our test server), `xraw.c` (one raw XPC request), `protos.m` (selectors + type encodings of the protocols: `protocols.txt`), `bp17x.py` (bplist17 decoder, `../iconservices/bp17.py` plus the one-byte `0xbN` objects), `labtest.sh` (FreeBSD lab VM).

## 1. Ranking: what a missing service does to the Dock's start

| service | Mac server (job, user) | call at start | blocking? | missing | rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `com.apple.coreservices.appleevents` | appleeventsd --server (LaunchDaemon, `_appleevents`) | `aeInitializeFromHIToolbox` -> check-in command 100, **synchronous** | yes, if the service exists and does not answer | error at once, AE goes on without a session (no Apple Events between processes) | 1 |
| `com.apple.ProgressReporting` | filecoordinationd (LaunchDaemon, root) | `DOCKDownloadDirectory` -> `addSubscriber:...fileURL:completionHandler:`, **synchronous** | yes, if the service exists and does not answer | error at once, no download progress on the Downloads stack | 2 |
| `com.apple.usernoted.dock` | usernoted (LaunchAgent, KeepAlive) | `DockNotificationCenterBadgeConnection` -> message_type 1, one-way | no | NSLog "Couldn't connect to the usernoted", no push badges; reconnects only on `com.apple.usernoted.isAlive` | 3 |
| `com.apple.coreservices.lsbestappsuggestionmanager.xpc` | useractivityd (LaunchAgent) | `startListeningForBestAppSuggestions` -> `doRegisterForBestAppChangeNotification`, one-way; `-bestAppSuggestion` is synchronous | only `-bestAppSuggestion` | no Handoff tile; a refused client reconnects for ever | 4 |
| `com.apple.dmd.policy` | dmd (LaunchAgent, Aqua) | `+[DMFApplicationPolicyMonitor createPolicyMonitorWithPolicyChangeHandler:completionHandler:]` -> `addRegistration:replyHandler:`, async | no | completion with NSCocoaErrorDomain 4097: Screen Time off, no dimmed tiles | 5 |
| `com.apple.carboncore.csnameddata` | csnameddatad.xpc (CarbonCore XPC service, per user) | `_CSSetNamedData("com.apple.dock,anwrite.<uid>")`, one-way | no (`_CSCopyNamedData` is synchronous, not at start) | the stamp is lost | 6 |
| `com.apple.analyticsd` | analyticsd (LaunchDaemon, `_analyticsd`) | `analytics_is_event_used` / `analytics_send_event` (async with reply) | no | events dropped | 7 |

**[decompiled]** sources of the "call at start" column: Dock `FUN_10000d672` (`aeInitializeFromHIToolbox(&{version 1, zeroed})`, then `AEInstallEventHandler('aevt','quit')` and `('SELF','test')`), `DOCKDownloadDirectory::initWithHandler:andPath:` (addSubscriberForFileURL), `DockNotificationCenterBadgeConnection::_serverConnection`, `FUN_100093c8b` (UABestAppSuggestionManager alloc/init, setDelegate:, startListeningForBestAppSuggestions), `ECScreenTimeController` init `FUN_1001d93df`, `SystemApplicationUninstaller` init `FUN_100243f0f`, `FUN_100314697` (analytics: `analytics_is_event_used` before every send). None of the eight explains "no bar" by itself: two are synchronous only when the name exists and the server does not answer (our launchd keeps a look-up of a job that never checks in waiting: those two must answer), the others are asynchronous or one-way.  All eight now answer on pearOS.

## 2. com.apple.usernoted.dock (the badges)

Server: `/usr/sbin/usernoted` (`usernoted/DockConnection.swift`), job `com.apple.usernoted` (the user's, RunAtLoad, KeepAlive; 12 Mach services, `.dock` is one).  Not the GNUstep `d/bsd/usernoted`.  Raw XPC, **not** NSXPC.

Client (Dock, Objective-C, **[decompiled]**):
- `_serverConnection`: `xpc_connection_create_mach_service("com.apple.usernoted.dock", main queue, 0)`, observes the distributed notification `com.apple.usernoted.isAlive` (selector `_got_server_keep_alive:`, which calls `_serverConnection` again), resumes, sends `_send_connection_authentication` = `{message_type: 1}` (uint64).  The flag "send authentication again" is set on `XPC_ERROR_CONNECTION_INTERRUPTED` ("Lost connection with usernoted."); `XPC_ERROR_CONNECTION_INVALID` logs "Couldn't connect to the usernoted" and nothing reconnects until the isAlive notification.
- `tellNCAboutDeletedApp:` `{message_type 2, bundle_identifier}`, `tellNCAboutUpdatedApp:` 3, `tellNCAboutBadgeReset:` 4 (one-way).
- Event handler `FUN_10015d512`: dictionary `message_type` 1 -> `_handleDockBadgeSet:` (`bundle_identifier` string, `badge_count` uint64: 0 removes the label, otherwise `NSNumberFormatter localizedStringFromNumber:numberStyle:1` -> `ECStatusLabelDescription initWithDefaultPositioningAndString:` on the tile, `setStatusLabel:forPushNotificationWithBundleID:`); 2 -> `_handleDisabledBadgesSet:` (`bundle_identifiers` array -> a set, `ECStatusLabelEnabledChangeNotification` posted for every identifier that entered or left the set; `badgesDisabledForIdentifier:` compares lowercased).

Server (usernoted, Swift, **[decompiled]**): the listener of `.dock` makes a `DockConnection`; its init checks the peer with `SecCode` `kSecCodeInfoIdentifier` compared **case-insensitively with "com.apple.dock"** (`FUN_1000d7c70`); a peer that fails gets nil: the connection is never resumed, its messages get nothing.  The newest Dock replaces the previous one (`Server+0x28`).  Message dispatch `FUN_10000e304`: 1 -> send `{message_type 2, bundle_identifiers: Server._appsThatCantBadge}` (always, also empty) then `{message_type 1, bundle_identifier, badge_count}` for every application with `_lastBadgeCount > 0`; 2 deleted (drops the app's records), 3 updated, 4 reset (`"Reset badge count for %s"`, `UPDATE app SET badge=NULL WHERE identifier=?`); any other value: Swift `fatalError` ("usernoted/DockConnection.swift" line 30).  Identifiers are lowercased.

**[captured]**: an unsigned probe gets nothing; the same probe signed ad hoc with `-i com.apple.dock` gets `{message_type 2, bundle_identifiers: [33 identifiers]}` (com.apple.clock, com.apple.reminders, com.apple.mail, ... on this Mac); no badge > 0 was set.  `killall usernoted` -> the new instance posts `com.apple.usernoted.isAlive` (no object).  Store: `~/Library/Group Containers/group.com.apple.usernoted/db2/db` (SQLite, `app(app_id, identifier, badge)`), settings `group.com.apple.usernoted.plist` (`apps`: bundle-id, flags, auth, ...).  **Open**: how `_appsThatCantBadge` is derived from `auth`/`flags` (tried `auth & 1 && flags & 8`: 13 of 51 entries disagree).

pearOS (`d/darwin/usernoted`, C): the same check (signing identifier via `common/csent.c`, `csent_identifier`), the same answers, badges read from Apple's store, reset written there, isAlive posted at start; the "may not badge" set is empty (no notification settings on pearOS); an unknown message_type is logged, not fatal.  **[captured]** on the Mac: our daemon (job `com.pearos.t.un`) gives the Dock-signed probe `{2, []}` then the badges of the store, ignores the unsigned probe, posts isAlive at start.

## 3. com.apple.ProgressReporting (NSProgress registry)

Server: `/usr/sbin/filecoordinationd`, job `com.apple.FileCoordination` (root; also `com.apple.FileCoordination` and host special port 30).  Foundation's client looks it up in the **system** domain (a LaunchAgent of the same name is not found: "Connection invalid").  NSXPC, protocol `NSProgressRegistrar` (signatures `protocols.txt`), callbacks `NSProgressSubscriber`, `NSProgressPublisher`.

**[captured]** (Apple's Foundation as subscriber of `~/Downloads/` and as publisher of a file in it, Apple's server):
- subscriber -> `addSubscriber:(proxy pn 2) forID:<uuid> appBundleID:nil fileURL:file:///Users/alex/Downloads/ completionHandler:` sent **with a synchronous reply**; reply: empty (`v@?`).
- publisher -> `addPublisher:(proxy) forID:<uuid> acknowledgementAppBundleIDs:nil category:nil fileURL:<file> initialValues:<NSProgressValues: userInfo {NSProgressFileOperationKindKey: Downloading, NSProgressFileURLKey}, selfFraction 0/100, childFraction 0/1, kind NSProgressKindFile, ...> completionHandler:`; reply `(nil)` (`v@?@`); then the server calls the publisher's `appWithBundleID:@"com.apple.dock" didAcknowledgeWithSuccess:NO` (the real Dock is a subscriber of Downloads).
- server -> subscriber `addPublisher:(proxy pn 2 of the subscriber's own connection) forID:<publisher id> withValues:<the same NSProgressValues> isOld:NO`, then every `observePublisherForID:values:forKeys:` of the publisher (fractionCompleted + completedUnitCount, ... finished, totalUnitCount) and `removePublisherForID:`.
pearOS (`d/darwin/filecoordinationd`, Objective-C): the same, with **[inferred]** rules: a publisher matches a subscriber of its own URL, of its parent directory, or of its category; `isOld` YES for publishers older than the subscriber; the acknowledgement is sent for subscribers with an app bundle id (success = listed in acknowledgementAppBundleIDs).  A proxy cannot travel over another connection (Foundation throws, our first version crashed), so the subscriber gets a registry-side forwarder, which is what Apple's does too (pn 2 of the subscriber's connection).  **[captured]**: the decoded subscriber stream from our daemon (job `com.pearos.t.fcd`, LaunchDaemon) is identical to Apple's except for UUIDs.

## 4. com.apple.coreservices.appleevents (Apple Event check-in)

Server: `/System/Library/CoreServices/appleeventsd --server` (LaunchDaemon, `_appleevents`).  Raw XPC with reply; AE.framework connects with the privileged flag (system domain).  **[captured]**:
- `aeInitializeFromHIToolbox(table)` (the Dock passes a zeroed table with version 1; other values: "AE.framework: incompatible with current HIToolbox") sends `{command 100, appName, signature 0, needsProcessAssertions false, port <send right>}` synchronously; reply `{success true, supressOAPP false, session 0}`.
- After a `com.apple.coreservices.appleevents.startup` notify(3) post the client checks in again with `reconnect: true`; appleeventsd posts that name when it starts (seen after `killall appleeventsd`).
- `{command 9999}` and `{command 101}`: the client sees "Connection invalid"; no command: "Connection interrupted".
pearOS (`d/darwin/appleeventsd`, C): check-in answered as above, kept per pid; other commands cancel the connection (our client then sees "interrupted", not "invalid": difference kept as open); startup name posted.  Routing of events between processes is not done.

## 5. com.apple.analyticsd

Server: CoreAnalytics.framework/Support/analyticsd (LaunchDaemon, `_analyticsd`).  Raw XPC, string `command`; the client connects privileged.  **[captured]**: `{command "send", event, json, timestamp, format 0, priority 0}` -> `{}`; then the server pushes `{notification "configuration-change", configuration <shmem 163840>, configuration-size 163388}`; `{}` (no command) -> `{error "not entitled"}` (+ the same push); `bogus`, `checkin`, `event-used` from our unentitled probe: connection invalid.  The Dock checks `analytics_is_event_used` before each send (**[decompiled]** `FUN_100314697`).  pearOS (`d/darwin/analyticsd`, C): send -> `{}`, none -> not entitled, others cancel; no configuration push (Apple's format, nothing to configure).

## 6. com.apple.carboncore.csnameddata

Server: the XPC service `CarbonCore.framework/XPCServices/csnameddatad.xpc` (`CFBundleIdentifier com.apple.carboncore.csnameddata`, ServiceType User), reached with `xpc_connection_create` (**[captured]**: dcap `CONNECT xpcservice`).  NSXPC, `_CSNamedDataReadWriteProtocol`.  CarbonCore's API (**[decompiled]** argument use): `_CSSetNamedData(const char *name, int flags = 0, const void *bytes, unsigned length)` -> one-way `setNamedData:data:`; `_CSCopyNamedData(CFStringRef name, int flags = 0, CFErrorRef *)` -> synchronous `getNamedData:withReply:`.  **[captured]** answers: get of a missing name `(nil, nil)`; `setNamedData:data:withReply:` `(YES, nil)`; flush (also of a missing name) `(YES, nil)`; keys / items: everything stored; nil data: `(YES, nil)`.  On the Mac the store held `com.apple.dock,anwrite.501` (8 bytes, a CFAbsoluteTime), `com.apple.systemuiserver.mtwrite.501`, `com.apple.screencapture.mtwrite.501`.  pearOS (`d/darwin/csnameddatad`): same answers, memory store, a job of the user (no XPC service domain in d's launchd).  Same results from ours on the Mac.

## 7. com.apple.dmd.policy (Screen Time)

Server: `/usr/libexec/dmd` (LaunchAgent, Aqua).  NSXPC `DMFPolicyRemoteInterface`.  **[captured]**: Apple's daemon refuses a client without `com.apple.private.dmd.policy` (the Dock has it): the framework reports NSCocoaErrorDomain 4097.  `createPolicyMonitorWithPolicyChangeHandler:` sends `addRegistration:<DMFPolicyRegistration identifier "dmf.policy.monitor.app", policyTypes {applicationcategories, websites, categories, applications}>`; `requestPoliciesForBundleIdentifiers:` sends the array.  **[decompiled]** (DeviceManagement `___92+[DMFApplicationPolicyMonitor createPolicyMonitorWithPolicyChangeHandler:completionHandler:]_block_invoke`): completion `(monitor, [reply count] != 0, nil)` - an empty reply dictionary means "Screen Time not enabled".  **[inferred]**: the answers of Apple's daemon to an entitled client (cannot be observed: AMFI kills a test program with the private entitlement); pearOS answers an empty registration dictionary, `DMFPolicyOK` (0) per identifier, empty budgets.

## 8. com.apple.coreservices.lsbestappsuggestionmanager.xpc (Handoff)

Server: useractivityd (LaunchAgent).  NSXPC `UABestAppSuggestionManagerProtocol` / client `UABestAppSuggestionManagerResponseProtocol`.  **[captured]**: `startListeningForBestAppSuggestions` -> one-way `doRegisterForBestAppChangeNotification`; Apple's daemon closes the connection of a client without `com.apple.private.coreservices.lsuseractivityd.bestappsuggestion` and the framework sends the registration again and again (12 times in 2 s).  `-bestAppSuggestion` is synchronous.  The Dock removes its continuity tile when the suggestion is nil (**[decompiled]** `FUN_100093f4e`).  pearOS: "no suggestion" answers, never a notify, payload fetch -> NSCocoaErrorDomain 3328 (ours, **[inferred]**).

## 9. com.apple.uninstall.service

Server: `Uninstall.framework/Resources/uninstalld` (LaunchDaemon, root; also `com.apple.uninstalld`, the old Mach interface).  NSXPC `UNUninstallServiceProtocol` (`queueRequest:withPriority:reply:` `(BOOL, NSError)`, `abortRequest:reply:` `(BOOL)`) / client `UNUninstallClientProtocol`.  Entitlement `com.apple.private.uninstalld.client` (the Dock has it).  **[decompiled]** the Dock only calls `initWithDelegate:` at start; `queueRequest:error:` later.  **[captured]** against our daemon: `queueRequest:error:` sends asynchronously and returns YES at once, the payload is `UNUninstallClientRequest {requestID NSUUID, bundleURLs, forceQuitForegroundApps, preserveApplicationUserData}`.  pearOS: requests answered NO + NSCocoaErrorDomain 3328 (ours): removing applications is not implemented.

## 10. Verification

- Mac VM (Apple's clients against ours, jobs `com.pearos.t.*`, `IS_REDIRECT` + `dcap.dylib`): usernoted, filecoordinationd, appleeventsd, analyticsd, csnameddatad, dmd, useractivityd, uninstalld (sections above).
- FreeBSD lab VM (darwinator, the Mac's frameworks from the dyld cache, d's launchd starting every job on demand, `labtest.sh`, 2026-11-27): all eight answer Apple's clients as on the Mac: AE check-in through `aeInitializeFromHIToolbox` (needs launchservicesd: d's launchd keeps that name for its owner), CoreAnalytics send, `_CSSetNamedData`/`_CSCopyNamedData` and the whole named data protocol, `createPolicyMonitor` (Screen Time off, no error), UABestAppSuggestionManager registration and `bestAppSuggestion` nil, NSProgress publisher -> Downloads subscriber with its updates and removal, usernoted answering the Dock-signed probe and ignoring the unsigned one, UNUninstallClient connection.  Daemons built on FreeBSD by `d/darwin/build.sh` (llvm21).
- Not verified: the real Dock against our daemons on the Mac (SIP keeps the real Dock's look-ups on Apple's names); the Dock on pearOS with all eight (next step on the ISO test stack, not touched by this work).
